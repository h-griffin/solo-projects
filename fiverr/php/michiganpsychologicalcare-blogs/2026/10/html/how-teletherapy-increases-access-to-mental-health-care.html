How Teletherapy Increases Access to Mental Health Care

Mental health care has become more accessible over time as stigma has decreased. Teletherapy provides additional ways for people to seek support in their own home. It can be difficult for people to start therapy in general, and teletherapy provides another environment for people to feel more comfortable in therapy.

Making Mental Health Care More Accessible

Unfortunately, not everyone who needs mental health care can get it. Some common barriers to mental health care access are travel time to an office, busy work schedules, challenges with transportation, or lack of availability in a person’s area. Some people also might feel more comfortable in a virtual environment versus an office in person. Virtual therapy provides an alternative to people who are seeking mental health care in a remote environment. 

Understanding Teletherapy and How It Works

Teletherapy is defined as mental health counseling in a remote environment. Teletherapy sessions typically take place over a secure video or phone platform. Clients can choose to partake in therapy sessions from home or in any private location where they have access to the internet. Virtual therapy sessions follow the same structure as therapy sessions in an office. 

How Teletherapy Helps Reduce Barriers to Mental Health Care

Teletherapy can help people who would not usually be able to attend therapy appointments still have access to mental health care. Some people may not have access to transportation or may not have time to drive long distances to therapy. Depending on where a person lives, they may not have access to mental health providers that specialize in what they are struggling with. By opening up their search to include virtual therapy, people can expand their search to other qualified professionals who are licensed to provide care in their state. People who have busy work schedules or other responsibilities may not have the time to travel to therapy appointments. Eliminating travel time can help some people still attend therapy.

Benefits of Virtual Therapy

Some other remote therapy benefits are that people may not feel comfortable in therapy offices. Virtual therapy appointments can help people maintain therapy appointments more consistently, which helps their progress over time. Everyone’s experience and preferences with therapy will be different and take time to explore.

Everyone struggling with mental health struggles deserves mental health care. Teletherapy helps people who might not typically have access to mental health care. For some people, teletherapy can provide less travel time to therapy, additional options for providers, and more flexibility with appointments in a way that fits into their lives. Working with an online counselor can help people work through mental health struggles in a virtual environment. For people considering therapy, online therapy can provide a helpful step toward healing and recovery.
