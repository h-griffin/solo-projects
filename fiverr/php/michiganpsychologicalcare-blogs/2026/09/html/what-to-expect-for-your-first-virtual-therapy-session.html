What to Expect for Your First Virtual Therapy Session

Starting a therapy journey can be scary and intimidating for people who have never done it before. Therapy can be a helpful tool in managing the symptoms of mental health conditions and dealing with life changes and stressors. Virtual therapy provides another opportunity for people to participate in therapy from the comfort of their home. Online therapy is typically delivered through a secure video platform or phone call. The first virtual therapy session usually consists of therapists and clients getting to know each other and discussing the patient’s goals. Online counselors can answer any specific questions or concerns that clients might have regarding treatment. When people are thinking about starting virtual therapy, they do not have to have it all figured out before the first appointment. 

What is Virtual Therapy?
Virtual therapy is defined as mental health counseling taking place remotely by video or phone. Teletherapy provides flexibility for patients with busy schedules because they can connect to sessions anywhere instead of needing to drive to a traditional therapy office. The therapeutic conversation, relationship, and goals remain the main focus of sessions; the setting of sessions is just different than the traditional therapy office.

What Happens During Your First Virtual Therapy Session?

The first virtual therapy session will start with patients connecting to the designated virtual platform that therapists set up for them. The start of the session will serve as an opportunity for patients to check their audio and video connection. Patients can ask their therapist any questions they have about technology, session structure, their therapeutic approach, or anything else they want to know. It’s okay if people have virtual therapy expectations before they start sessions. Therapists can answer any questions during the first session.

How to Get the Most Out of Virtual Therapy

Patients can try to get the most out of their sessions by limiting distractions during teletherapy appointments. This may include turning off their phone notifications, taking time off work, or letting family members know they will not be available during appointment times. Some patients might find it helpful to take notes during or after sessions to write down important reflections.

Is Virtual Therapy Right for You?

Virtual therapy is good for people who are looking for a convenient and flexible way to pursue therapy. Teletherapy reduces travel time for patients and allows them to partake in therapy sessions in whatever environment they are most comfortable in. People who may feel uncomfortable in traditional therapy offices might find virtual therapy a good fit.

For some people, knowing what to expect before starting online counseling sessions helps it feel a little less scary. The first virtual therapy session serves as an opportunity for patients to meet their therapist, talk about their goals, and develop a treatment plan. Patients can ask any questions they have regarding the structure of sessions and what to expect moving forward.
